After Dark — an adults-only board game for couples

After Dark is a 18+ board game for two, played together on one phone. Roll the dice, move around the board, and take on the card you land on — then see who makes it home first.

Choose your heat level — Romantic, Sensual, or Spicy — add both your names, set a safe word, and exclude any card categories you don't want. Then play across a 30-space board with 80+ cards:

Kiss — slow, teasing, and close
Touch — hands-on, with an optional 60-second timer
Truth — honest questions for two
Dare — bold moves, with an optional 60-second timer
Fantasy — say it out loud

Chance and Breathe spaces mix things up along the way, and every card you complete earns you hearts. First player home wins the grand-finale card.

Passing or swapping any card is always free, and nothing is saved or sent — what happens in the game stays between the two of you.

18+ only. Play only with a consenting partner, and use your safe word
